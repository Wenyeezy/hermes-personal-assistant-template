# Community and feedback

Hermes Life is the primary public entry point. The most useful contribution
today is one concrete experience from trying its starter: where setup became
confusing, what you expected and what happened. Research Labs feedback is also
welcome, with the understanding that HIIS and Crypto + RWA are in development.

## Pick one path

| --- | --- |
| Understand the project | [Project map](docs/project-map.md) |

| Report an onboarding problem or bug | [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Wenyeezy/hermes-personal-assistant-template/issues/new/choose) |

For a useful report, say what you were trying to do, the exact public step you
followed, what you expected, and what happened. A fictional input is usually
enough; do not upload personal memory, account data, private documents or
unedited logs.

## How feedback becomes work

Questions start in Discussions. Reproducible problems become Issues with a
clear expected result. Changes link back to that problem, and a dated update
records what improved and what remains uncertain.

The proposed initial routine is one weekly feedback review. Response times are
not guaranteed. Early success means people can complete a useful task and
return with feedback, not merely accumulate reactions or stars.

Keep discussion respectful and specific. Challenge methods and claims rather
than people. Avoid unsolicited promotions and requests for private credentials.
